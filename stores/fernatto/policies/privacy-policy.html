Privacy policy

This policy explains how SANA PARTNERS LTD (company no. 13363014), Top Floor 19 Windsor Place, Cardiff, Wales, CF10 3BY, United Kingdom, trading as Fernatto ("we", "us"), collects and uses your personal data when you visit fernatto.com or buy from us. We are the controller of your personal data under the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR) and the Data Protection Act 2018.

Contact for privacy questions: contact@fernatto.com.

What we collect

  • Order and account data: name, billing and delivery address, email, phone number, order history, and account login details if you create an account.
  • Payment data: payments are processed by our payment providers. We do not see or store your full card details.
  • Communications: messages you send us and our replies.
  • Device and usage data: IP address, browser and device type, pages viewed and how you reached our site, collected through cookies and similar technologies.
  • Marketing preferences: whether you have agreed to receive marketing emails.

Why we use it and our legal basis

  • To process, deliver and support your order, handle returns and refunds — performance of a contract.
  • To meet tax, accounting and consumer-law obligations — legal obligation.
  • To keep our site secure, prevent fraud and improve our store — legitimate interests.
  • To send marketing emails and use non-essential cookies — consent, which you can withdraw at any time.

Who we share it with

We share personal data only as needed with service providers acting on our behalf: our e-commerce platform (Shopify), payment providers, delivery carriers, email service providers, and analytics and advertising providers (such as Google) where you have consented. We may also disclose data where required by law. We do not sell your personal data.

International transfers

Some providers, including Shopify, may process data outside the UK. Where they do, transfers are protected by UK adequacy regulations or appropriate safeguards such as the UK International Data Transfer Addendum.

Cookies

We use strictly necessary cookies to run the store (for example, your basket and checkout). With your consent we also use analytics and advertising cookies. You can change your choice at any time through the cookie banner or your browser settings.

How long we keep it

We keep order records for six years to meet UK tax and accounting rules. Account data is kept while your account is open. Marketing data is kept until you unsubscribe.

Your rights

You have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data, to restrict or object to its processing, to data portability, and to withdraw consent at any time. To use these rights, email contact@fernatto.com. We will respond within one month.

If you are unhappy with how we handle your data, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk or on 0303 123 1113.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The current version is always available on this page.
