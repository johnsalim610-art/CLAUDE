Returns & refunds

This policy applies to orders placed at fernatto.com, operated by SANA PARTNERS LTD (company no. 13363014), Top Floor 19 Windsor Place, Cardiff, Wales, CF10 3BY, United Kingdom.

Your 14-day right to return

You can return any item for any reason within 14 days of the day you (or someone you nominate) receive it. This is in addition to your statutory right to cancel under the Consumer Contracts (Information, Cancellation and Additional Charges) Regulations 2013, which also gives you 14 days from delivery to tell us you want to cancel.

Free returns

Returns are free: a prepaid return label is included in your parcel. There is no restocking fee.

How to return an item

  1. Email contact@fernatto.com with your order number and the item(s) you are returning. You can also use the model cancellation form below, but you don't have to.
  2. Pack the item securely, ideally in its original packaging, with all parts and accessories.
  3. Attach the prepaid return label from your parcel and drop it off. If you have lost the label, email us and we will send a new one free of charge.
  4. Please send the item back within 14 days of telling us you want to return it.

Condition of returned items

You may handle an item as you would in a shop to check its nature, characteristics and how it works. If an item has been handled beyond that and has lost value as a result, we may reduce your refund by that diminished value, as the law allows.

Exchanges

Exchanges are accepted within the same 14-day window. Email us with your order number and the item you would like instead; we will send the replacement once the return is on its way, subject to stock. If the new item costs more or less, we will charge or refund the difference.

Refunds

We refund to your original payment method within 5 business days of receiving your return (or, if earlier, receiving proof that you have sent it back). We refund the price of the item and the original delivery charge (UK delivery is free). We will email you when your refund is issued; your bank may take a few extra days to show it.

Faulty, damaged or incorrect items

If an item arrives damaged, faulty or not as described, please email us with your order number and a photo. Under the Consumer Rights Act 2015 you are entitled to a full refund within 30 days of delivery, or a repair or replacement after that. We always cover return costs for faulty items. Nothing in this policy affects your statutory rights.

Items that cannot be returned

Personalised or made-to-order items, and sealed goods that are not suitable for return for health or hygiene reasons once unsealed, cannot be returned unless they are faulty.
